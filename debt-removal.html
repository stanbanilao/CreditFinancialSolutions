Debt review removal

Clear the path after debt review.

Debt review removal is the formal process used to update your credit profile once you satisfy the applicable requirements. Clearline Financial Solutions helps you understand what is required and what should happen next.

Eligibility first

Before anything is removed, your status must be assessed.

Consumers often use “debt removal” to mean removing a debt review indicator from a credit profile. The correct route depends on the facts of your case, including whether debts under the process have been settled and whether the relevant legal requirements have been met.

What you may need

Prepare the right information from the start.

ID

Identity Details

Information needed to identify the correct consumer record and avoid mismatches.

PU

Paid-Up Letters

Settlement confirmations from credit providers may be required as evidence that qualifying accounts are settled.

CR

Credit Record Information

Your current credit profile can help show what status or adverse information is still reflected.

Important

Debt review removal is not a shortcut to erase lawful debt.
